How it works

  1. Download prices. About 12 years of daily open, high, low, close and volume for each stock, plus the S&P 500 and the VIX fear index, from Yahoo Finance.
  2. Turn prices into clues. 15 per stock per day, the same things a trader eyeballs: recent moves, distance from the 20, 50 and 200-day averages, RSI, volatility, volume spikes and what the whole market is doing.
  3. Train on the past. A logistic regression learns from 2014 to 2022 how often stocks with clues like these closed higher a week later. A fancier gradient boosting model was tested too and did worse.
  4. Test on data it never saw. Every check on this page uses 2023 onward, with a gap so no training answers leak into the test.
  5. Predict. The final model is retrained on everything and scores today's clues. The data on this page refreshes every weekday after the market closes.

Built by Gurutej Bandla. Code and full write-up on GitHub.

A learning project, not financial advice. Small edges like these are easy to lose to trading costs, taxes and bad luck.
